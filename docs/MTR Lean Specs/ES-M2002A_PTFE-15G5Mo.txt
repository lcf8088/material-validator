## Scope

Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), 15% glass and 5% molybdenum disulfide filled.

## Chemical Composition

Compound composition is proprietary. Material shall conform to the industry standard for glass/MoS2-filled PTFE per supplier's compound data sheet.

## Heat Treatment Condition

Not applicable (thermoplastic material).

## Mechanical Properties

| Property                  | Minimum    | Maximum    | Test Method        |
|---------------------------|------------|------------|--------------------|
| Tensile Strength at Break | 17 MPa     | -          | ASTM D638          |
| Elongation at Break       | 200%       | -          | ASTM D638          |
| Hardness                  | 60         | 68         | ASTM D2240 Shore D |
| Specific Gravity          | 2.20 g/cm³ | 2.30 g/cm³ | ASTM D792          |

Note: PTFE does not exhibit a distinct yield point per ASTM D638; tensile strength and elongation are reported at break only. Modulus of elasticity is excluded per risk assessment — for filled PTFE compounds, modulus is governed by filler content, which is verified by specific gravity and hardness. Anomalous modulus cannot occur when both are in specification.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is required, stating batch number, cure date, and compound identification.
