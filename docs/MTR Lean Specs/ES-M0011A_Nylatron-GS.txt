## Scope

Nylatron GS PA 66 + MoS2 (extruded, MoS2-filled Nylon 6/66) thermoplastic.

## Chemical Composition

Compound composition is proprietary. Material shall conform to the industry standard for Nylatron GS PA 66 + MoS2 per supplier's compound data sheet.

## Heat Treatment Condition

Not applicable (thermoplastic material).
