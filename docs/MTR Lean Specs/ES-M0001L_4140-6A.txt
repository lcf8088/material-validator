## Scope

API 6A supplement to ES-M0001C (AISI 4140, UNS G41400). This specification adds requirements for materials used in API 6A equipment (blind flanges). All requirements of ES-M0001C apply; this supplement defines additional acceptance criteria per API Specification 6A.

## Base Specification

ES-M0001C — 4140 Low-Alloy Steel. All chemical composition, heat treatment condition, and receiving requirements per the base specification.

## API 6A Material Designation

75K per API 6A Table 7 / Table 8.

## Additional Mechanical Properties

The following properties are required in addition to those in ES-M0001C:

| Property              | Minimum | Maximum | Test Method    |
|-----------------------|---------|---------|----------------|
| Reduction in Area     | 35%     | -       | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Hardness (Brinell)    | HBW 197 | -       | ASTM E10       |

## Chemical Composition — 6A Additional Limits

In addition to ES-M0001C chemistry, the following API 6A Table 11 / Table 12 limits apply:

| Element | Maximum (%) |
|---------|-------------|
| V       | 0.30        |

All other Table 11 and Table 12 PSL 1 limits are satisfied by the base specification.

## Heat Treatment Process

Quenched and Tempered per ANSI/NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

- Hardening temperature: 850°C to 895°C (1562°F to 1643°F)
- Soak time: 1/2 hour per inch of maximum through thickness, 1 hour minimum
- Quench media: Water, oil, or other quenching media
- Water quench temperature: shall not exceed 38°C (100°F) at start, 49°C (120°F) at completion
- Tempering temperature: 660°C (1220°F) minimum
- Temper time: 3/4 hour per inch of maximum through thickness, 1 hour minimum

## Melting Practice

Material shall be produced by Electric Arc Furnace (EAF), Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF), or equivalent. The MTR shall state the melting practice used.

## Material Qualification

Mechanical properties shall be determined from a qualification test coupon (QTC) from the same heat of material, hot worked and heat treated with the production parts it qualifies. Tensile specimens shall be removed from the center core ¼T envelope. QTC equivalent round shall be equal to or greater than the production part equivalent round. Hardness testing shall be performed on the QTC after final heat treatment.

## Forming Practices

Material shall be produced using hot working practices that produce a wrought structure throughout.

## NDE Requirements

Visual inspection is required for each part and is performed during receiving inspection per FT.021.

## Heat-Treating Equipment

Heat-treating equipment shall be calibrated per FT.103.

## Receiving

In addition to ES-M0001C receiving requirements, the MTR shall include reduction in area, Brinell hardness, and a statement of melting practice.
