## Scope

AISI 1075 carbon steel for disc springs (Belleville washers). Chemistry-only specification; no incoming mechanical property acceptance criteria.

## Chemical Composition

| Element | Minimum (%) | Maximum (%) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| C       | 0.70        | 0.80        |
| Mn      | 0.40        | 0.70        |
| P       | -           | 0.040       |
| S       | -           | 0.050       |

## Heat Treatment Condition

Not applicable. Material is purchased as finished disc springs from catalog suppliers.

## Mechanical Properties

No incoming mechanical property acceptance criteria. Mechanical properties are inherent to the finished spring product as supplied.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is required, stating material grade and lot or batch identification.
