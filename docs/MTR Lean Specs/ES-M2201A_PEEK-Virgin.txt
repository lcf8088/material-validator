## Scope

Virgin Polyetheretherketone (PEEK).

## Chemical Composition

Compound composition is proprietary. Material shall conform to the industry standard for virgin PEEK per supplier's compound data sheet.

## Heat Treatment Condition

Not applicable (thermoplastic material).

## Mechanical Properties

| Property                  | Minimum     | Maximum | Test Method        |
|---------------------------|-------------|---------|--------------------|
| Tensile Strength at Yield | 13,000 psi  | -       | ASTM D638          |
| Elongation at Break       | 30%         | -       | ASTM D638          |
| Modulus of Elasticity     | 430,000 psi | -       | ASTM D638          |
| Hardness                  | 82          | 88      | ASTM D2240 Shore D |
| Specific Gravity          | 1.28        | 1.32    | ASTM D792          |

Note: Tensile strength at break and elongation at yield are excluded per risk assessment. For ductile PEEK, tensile strength at yield is the maximum engineering stress and the controlling acceptance criterion; tensile strength at break (post-necking) is lower per ASTM D638 for ductile thermoplastics, and API 14L permits "at yield or at break." Elongation at yield is a dependent property governed by tensile strength and modulus — anomalous yield elongation cannot occur when both are in specification.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is required, stating batch number, cure date, and compound identification.
