## Scope

Stainless steel and alloy steel fasteners. Material grade, class, and condition shall be as specified on the purchase order.

## Acceptance Standards

| Fastener Type | Acceptance Standard |
|---------------|---------------------|
| SS Socket Button and Flat Countersunk Head Cap Screws | ASTM F879 |
| SS Socket, Square Head, and Slotted Headless Set Screws | ASTM F880 |
| Alloy Steel Socket Set Screws | ASTM F912 |
| SS Bolts, Hex Cap Screws, Studs | ASTM F593 |
| SS Nuts | ASTM F594 |
| Alloy Steel Socket Head Cap Screws | ASTM A574 |

Chemical composition, mechanical properties, and hardness requirements are defined by the applicable ASTM standard. No additional acceptance criteria beyond the referenced standard.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is required, referencing the applicable ASTM standard, grade/class, and lot identification. The CoC shall be endorsed by authorized personnel.

## Sour Service Requirements

When sour service is specified on the purchase order or work order, fasteners shall additionally conform to the following:

**Alloy Steel Bolts/Studs:**
- ASTM A193 grade B7M or ASTM A320 grade L7M only
- Quenched and Tempered
- 22 HRC maximum
- Less than 1% nickel

**Alloy Steel Nuts:**
- ASTM A194 grade 2HM or 7M only
- 22 HRC maximum

**Austenitic Stainless Steel Fasteners:**
- Solution Annealed condition
- Standard austenitic grades (e.g., 304, 316) are acceptable for downhole hardware without additional environmental limits

**Receiving — Sour Service:**
A Material Test Report (MTR) is required in addition to the CoC. The MTR shall include grade, heat number, chemical analysis, mechanical properties, hardness, and heat treatment condition.
