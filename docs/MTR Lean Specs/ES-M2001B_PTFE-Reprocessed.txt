## Scope

Re-Processed Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE).

## Chemical Composition

Compound composition is proprietary. Material shall conform to the industry standard for re-processed PTFE per supplier's compound data sheet.

## Heat Treatment Condition

Not applicable (thermoplastic material).

## Mechanical Properties

| Property                  | Minimum    | Maximum | Test Method        |
|---------------------------|------------|---------|--------------------|
| Tensile Strength at Break | 10 MPa     | -       | ASTM D638          |
| Elongation at Break       | 75%        | -       | ASTM D638          |
| Hardness                  | -          | -       | ASTM D2240 Shore D |
| Specific Gravity          | 2.16 g/cm³ | -       | ASTM D792          |

Note: PTFE does not exhibit a distinct yield point per ASTM D638; tensile strength and elongation are reported at break only. Modulus of elasticity is excluded per risk assessment — modulus is a compound-level property governed by composition, verified by specific gravity and hardness. Anomalous modulus cannot occur when these properties are in specification. Hardness value to be set from supplier COA.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is required, stating batch number, cure date, and compound identification.
