## Scope

AISI 4340 (UNS G43400) low-alloy steel.

## Chemical Composition

| Element | Minimum (%) | Maximum (%) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| C       | 0.38        | 0.43        |
| Mn      | 0.60        | 0.80        |
| Cr      | 0.70        | 0.90        |
| Mo      | 0.20        | 0.30        |
| P       | -           | 0.025       |
| S       | -           | 0.025       |
| Si      | 0.15        | 0.35        |
| Ni      | 1.65        | 2.00        |

## Heat Treatment Condition

Quenched and Tempered.

## Mechanical Properties

Mechanical properties shall be verified by tests conducted on a representative sample from the same heat, size, and heat treat lot. Material stress relieved after cold working in excess of 5% is exempt from re-testing provided the stress relief temperature does not exceed the original tempering temperature.

| Property         | Minimum  | Maximum  | Test Method    |
|------------------|----------|----------|----------------|
| Yield Strength   | 150 ksi  | -        | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Tensile Strength | 170 ksi  | -        | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Elongation       | 12%      | -        | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Hardness         | -        | 45 HRC   | ASTM E18       |

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) and Material Test Report (MTR) are required; the MTR shall include grade, heat number, material condition, chemical analysis, mechanical properties, and endorsement by authorized personnel.
