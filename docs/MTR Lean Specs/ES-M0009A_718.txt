## Scope

Alloy 718 (UNS N07718) nickel-chromium precipitation-hardenable alloy, suitable for sour service per ANSI/NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

## Chemical Composition

| Element | Minimum | Maximum |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Ni      | 50.0%   | 55.0%   |
| Cr      | 17.0%   | 21.0%   |
| Mo      | 2.80%   | 3.30%   |
| Nb      | 4.75%   | 5.50%   |
| Ti      | 0.65%   | 1.15%   |
| Al      | 0.20%   | 0.80%   |
| Fe      | Balance | -       |
| S       | -       | 0.015%  |

## Heat Treatment Condition

Solution Annealed and Aged per ANSI/NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

## Mechanical Properties

Mechanical properties shall be verified by tests conducted on a representative sample from the same heat, size, and heat treat lot. Material stress relieved after cold working in excess of 5% is exempt from re-testing provided the stress relief temperature does not exceed the original tempering temperature.

| Property         | Minimum  | Maximum | Test Method    |
|------------------|----------|---------|----------------|
| Yield Strength   | 120 ksi  | -       | ASTM E8        |
| Tensile Strength | 185 ksi  | -       | ASTM E8        |
| Elongation       | 12%      | -       | ASTM E8        |
| Hardness         | -        | 40 HRC  | ASTM E18       |
| Charpy V-Notch at -75°F (-59°C) | 35 ft-lb avg | - | ASTM E23 |

No individual Charpy impact value shall fall below 30 ft-lb. Minimum lateral expansion shall be 15 mils.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) and Material Test Report (MTR) are required; the MTR shall include grade, heat number, chemical analysis, mechanical properties, hardness, and endorsement by authorized personnel.
