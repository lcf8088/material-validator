## Scope

AISI 8620 (UNS G86200) low-alloy steel.

## Chemical Composition

| Element | Minimum (%) | Maximum (%) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| C       | 0.18        | 0.23        |
| Mn      | 0.70        | 0.90        |
| P       | -           | 0.035       |
| S       | -           | 0.040       |
| Si      | 0.15        | 0.35        |
| Ni      | 0.40        | 0.70        |
| Cr      | 0.40        | 0.60        |
| Mo      | 0.15        | 0.25        |

## Heat Treatment Condition

As supplied (no specific heat treatment condition required). Material is typically purchased for subsequent case hardening by the manufacturer.

## Mechanical Properties

Chemistry-only specification. No incoming mechanical property acceptance criteria. Mechanical properties are established by the manufacturer's subsequent case hardening process.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) and Material Test Report (MTR) are required; the MTR shall include grade, heat number, chemical analysis, and endorsement by authorized personnel.
