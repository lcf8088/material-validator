## Scope

iglide T500 thermoplastic bearing material.

## Chemical Composition

Compound composition is proprietary. Material shall conform to the industry standard for iglide T500 per supplier's material data sheet.

## Heat Treatment Condition

Not applicable (thermoplastic material).

## Mechanical Properties

Mechanical properties shall conform to the iglide T500 supplier's published material data sheet. The supplier data sheet is adopted as the controlled acceptance standard for this material.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is required, stating batch number and compound identification as applicable.
