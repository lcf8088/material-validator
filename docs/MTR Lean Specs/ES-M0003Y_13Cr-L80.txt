## Scope

API 5CT 13Cr L80 (UNS S42000) martensitic stainless steel, suitable for sour service per ANSI/NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

## Chemical Composition

| Element | Minimum (%) | Maximum (%) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| C       | 0.15        | 0.22        |
| Mn      | 0.25        | 1.00        |
| Cr      | 12.00       | 14.00       |
| Mo      | -           | 0.50        |
| Ni      | -           | 0.50        |
| P       | -           | 0.020       |
| S       | -           | 0.010       |

## Heat Treatment Condition

Quenched and Tempered per ANSI/NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

## Mechanical Properties

Mechanical properties shall be verified by tests conducted on a representative sample from the same heat, size, and heat treat lot. Material stress relieved after cold working in excess of 5% is exempt from re-testing provided the stress relief temperature does not exceed the original tempering temperature.

| Property         | Minimum | Maximum | Test Method    |
|------------------|---------|---------|----------------|
| Yield Strength   | 80 ksi  | 95 ksi  | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Tensile Strength | 95 ksi  | -       | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Elongation       | 13%     | -       | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Hardness         | -       | 22 HRC  | ASTM E18       |

Charpy impact testing shall be performed per ASTM E23. Test temperature shall be 0°C (32°F) or lower. Transverse orientation is required; longitudinal acceptable when transverse is not possible. No individual specimen shall exhibit absorbed energy below two-thirds of the required minimum average.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) and Material Test Report (MTR) are required; the MTR shall include grade, heat number, chemical analysis, mechanical properties, material condition, and endorsement by authorized personnel.
