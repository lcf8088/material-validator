## Scope

Tungsten Carbide (Grade C2), Grades 10, 15, and 25.

## Chemical Composition

| Component        | Minimum (%) | Maximum (%) |
|------------------|-------------|-------------|
| Tungsten Carbide | 93          | 95          |
| Cobalt binder    | 5           | 7           |

## Condition

Sintered.

## Mechanical Properties

Mechanical properties shall be verified by tests conducted on a representative sample from the same production lot.

| Property                    | Minimum   | Maximum   | Test Method |
|-----------------------------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Transverse Rupture Strength | 220 ksi   | -         | ASTM B406   |
| Hardness                    | 90.0 HRA  | 92.5 HRA  | ASTM B294   |

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) and Material Test Report (MTR) are required; the MTR shall include grade, heat number, chemical analysis, hardness, and mechanical properties when available.
