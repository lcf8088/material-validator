## Scope

Super 13Cr martensitic stainless steel, restricted to UNS S41425, S41426, S41427, JFE KO-HP2-13CR-95, and NKK NK-CR13S-95, suitable for sour service per ANSI/NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

## Heat Treatment Condition

Quenched and Tempered per ANSI/NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

## Mechanical Properties

Mechanical properties shall be verified by tests conducted on a representative sample from the same heat, size, and heat treat lot. Material stress relieved after cold working in excess of 5% is exempt from re-testing provided the stress relief temperature does not exceed the original tempering temperature.

| Property (All UNS) | Minimum | Maximum | Test Method    |
|---------------------|---------|---------|----------------|
| Yield Strength      | 95 ksi  | -       | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Tensile Strength    | 105 ksi | -       | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Elongation          | 15%     | -       | ASTM A370 / E8 |
| Charpy V-Notch at +14°F (-10°C) | 44 ft-lb avg | - | ASTM E23 |

No individual Charpy impact value shall fall below 29.5 ft-lb, and no more than one of three results may be below the 44 ft-lb minimum average.

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) and Material Test Report (MTR) are required; the MTR shall include grade, heat number, chemical analysis, condition, mechanical properties, and endorsement by authorized personnel.
