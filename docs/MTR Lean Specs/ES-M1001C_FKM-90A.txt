## Scope

FKM (Viton) fluorocarbon elastomer, 90 durometer.

## Chemical Composition

Compound composition is proprietary. Material shall conform to the industry standard for FKM (Viton) per supplier's compound data sheet.

## Heat Treatment Condition

Not applicable (elastomer material).

## Mechanical Properties

| Property               | Minimum    | Maximum | Test Method          |
|------------------------|------------|---------|----------------------|
| Tensile Strength at Break | 1,450 psi  | -       | ASTM D412            |
| Elongation at Break    | 100%       | -       | ASTM D412            |
| Hardness (Shore A)     | 85         | 95      | ASTM D2240           |
| Compression Set        | Report     | -       | ASTM D395            |
| Tensile Modulus (100%) | Report     | -       | ASTM D412            |
| Specific Gravity       | Report     | -       | ASTM D792            |

## Receiving

A Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is required, stating batch number, cure date, and compound identification.
